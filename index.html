Welcome to

Shalgar Villa

Nestled in the picturesque Petri area, just 136 km from Pune International Airport, Shalgar Villa offers a sanctuary of peace and natural elegance. Our property features a beautiful garden, an outdoor fireplace, and breathtaking 180-degree valley views.

Enjoy our family-friendly restaurant serving Chinese, British, Indian, Italian, seafood, and local cuisines. Whether you're relaxing in our shared lounge, enjoying the game room, or exploring the Kaas Plateau, we promise a comfortable and memorable stay with top-tier amenities.
